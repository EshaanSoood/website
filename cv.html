Tours and Performances

Featured at the Rochester International Jazz Fest as well as 2 East coast tours spanning Weschester, Boston, Rochester, New York City and Washington DC. Featured at venues such as The Music Matters Festival in Singapore, The Pianoman Jazz Club in New Delhi, The International Club in Bangladesh, Shapeshifter Lab in New York City.

Music Director - The Greenpoint Gallery

The Gallery hosts weekly art shows with live music. My role is to curate and facilitate 8 live acts every week across the two stages at the gallery.

October 2, 2025

JW Vibe by Jonathan Widran

“Dream River offers very personal musical glimpses into Sood’s life experiences and mindset as a musician thriving in the U.S. These include the locomotive coolness and lyrical charm of “Glass Blown Acquaintances” (his interpretation of a fascinating conversation he once had with a passenger), the haunting and elegant “Miss Lightning” (a reflection on the passing of his beloved dog), a “Plea for Forgiveness” that is alternately sweetly tender and emphatically explosive, and the jamming, high energy and playfully thumping “Here For a Good Time,” which finds him inventively setting Coltrane changes to infectious dance grooves.”

https://www.jwvibe.com/single-post/eshaan-sood-the-sonic-alchemists-dream-river

September 25, 2025

Jazz Views (UK) by Tim Larsen

“Guitarist and composer Eshaan Sood makes a strong debut with Dream River. These pieces are lyrical, patient, often impressionistic, closer in spirit to storytelling, with voices overlapping and blending instead of lining up for solos. What ties it all together is Sood’s knack for telling stories through the band as a whole. He plays plenty, but he’s just as willing to let his bandmates share the spotlight. That sense of shared storytelling is what makes Dream River such a rewarding debut.”

https://jazzviews.net/eshaan-sood-the-sonic-alchemists-dream-river/
